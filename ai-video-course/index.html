A clear way to learn

A good AI video starts before you press generate.

Beautiful clips are only part of the work. You also need a strong idea, a plan for your scenes and the skills to bring everything together.

STEP 01

Develop the idea

Decide what the video should say, who it is for and what each scene needs to communicate.

STEP 02

Create the scenes

Turn your plan into useful prompts, generate footage and improve visual consistency.

STEP 03

Finish the video

Arrange your clips, refine the pace and add sound, dialogue and finishing details.
