Short biography

Sir Oyedot AI Creator is the creative name of Oyedotun Opeyemi Stephen, a Nigerian AI video creator, video editor, cinematic storyteller and educator based in Ibadan, Oyo State. His work includes AI-assisted historical films, documentary-style wildlife storytelling, short-form advertisements and educational videos. He combines a background in mathematics and science education with visual storytelling.

Education

2024: Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) in Mathematics/Integrated Science Education, Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria.

2017: Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) in Integrated Science/Mathematics, Adeyemi College of Education, Ondo, Nigeria.

Teaching career

His curriculum vitae records teaching roles dating from 2013, including Abake Model College (2021–2025), His Banner Premier College (2021–2025), New Hope Group of Schools (2016–2020) and Excellent Kiddies Nursery and Primary School (2013–2014).

Professional memberships

Registered teacher, Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN). Member, Integrated Science Educators Association of Nigeria (ISEAN). These affiliations are self-reported in his CV.
