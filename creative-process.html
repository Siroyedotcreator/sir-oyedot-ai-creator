BEHIND THE CREATIVE PROCESS

From an idea to the screen.

A look at the creative disciplines involved in AI-assisted filmmaking: storytelling, visual planning, consistency, editing and publishing. This is a process overview, not a claim that behind-the-scenes footage has been released.

01 / STORY

Concept and research

Define the story, audience and creative direction. Cultural stories need careful distinction between inspiration, dramatization and documented history.

02 / PREPRODUCTION

Scene and character planning

Plan locations, visual references, camera perspectives, character appearance and the continuity needed across shots.

03 / VISUAL PRODUCTION

AI-assisted scene creation

Develop visual sequences, assess realism and composition, and refine material to support the story rather than distract from it.

04 / POSTPRODUCTION

Editing and finishing

Bring scenes together through pacing, sound, transitions and final quality checks for a coherent viewing experience.
