The educator and the creator

I'm a Nigerian video creator and educator based in Ibadan, Oyo State. I work with AI-assisted video tools to build cinematic scenes, historical stories, wildlife documentaries, creative advertisements and educational content.

Before my creative work in AI video, I built a career in teaching. My classroom experience includes Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Basic Science and Economics across primary and secondary school levels.

I care about how ideas are explained. In teaching, that means making difficult topics understandable. In filmmaking, it means finding the right image, pacing and story so an audience can follow along.
